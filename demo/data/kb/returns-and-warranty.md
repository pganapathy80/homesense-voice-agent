# Returns and warranty

**Returns.** Any HomeSense device can be returned within 30 days of delivery for
a full refund, in any condition. We email a prepaid label; refunds land 5 to 7
business days after the device arrives at the warehouse.

**Standard warranty.** Every device carries a one-year limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. Accidental damage, water damage on non-rated devices and
normal battery wear are not covered.

**Extended warranty.** Active Premium Cloud Video subscribers get two years
instead of one, for as long as the plan stays active.

**Replacements.** For a device that fails under warranty we ship a replacement
before the faulty unit is returned. A prepaid label is included; the faulty unit
must be sent back within 21 days.

Refunds and cancellations are handled by a human specialist, not by the voice
agent.
