# Camera showing offline

A HomeSense camera reports offline when it has not reached the cloud for more
than two minutes.

1. **Check power.** The status LED should be solid green. No LED means no power
   at the outlet or the USB-C cable has worked loose.
2. **Check Wi-Fi strength.** In the app, open the camera and tap Signal. Below
   two bars, move the camera closer to the router or add a hub extender.
3. **Power-cycle.** Unplug for 15 seconds, then plug back in. Allow 90 seconds
   to rejoin.
4. **Re-pair.** If it is still offline, remove the camera in the app and add it
   again with the QR code on the base.

Recording gaps during an outage cannot be recovered; cloud video only stores
what reached the cloud. If the camera stays offline after re-pairing, we open a
support ticket.
