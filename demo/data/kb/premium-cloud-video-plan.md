# Premium Cloud Video plan

This is the HomeSense subscription. Customers ask about it as the plan, the
subscription, or cloud storage.

**Price.** The plan costs 14.99 US dollars per month, or 149 dollars per year. Billed to the
card on file and renews automatically.

**What is included.**

- 60 days of cloud video history for every camera on the account
- 24/7 professional monitoring with dispatch
- Smart alerts: person, vehicle, package and animal detection
- Unlimited camera count on one address
- Extended two-year warranty on all HomeSense devices while the plan is active

**Without the plan** cameras still stream live and record locally for 24 hours,
but there is no cloud history, no professional monitoring and no smart alerts.

**If a plan lapses** recordings are retained for 30 days, then deleted.
Reactivating within 30 days restores access to that history.
