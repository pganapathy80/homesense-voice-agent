# Door lock battery

The HomeSense smart lock uses four AA batteries and typically runs 9 to 12
months. Lithium AA cells last longest in cold weather; do not use rechargeable
NiMH, which read as low almost immediately.

**Low battery warning.** At 20 percent the keypad flashes amber after each
unlock and the app sends a notification. At 5 percent the lock stops using the
motor for auto-lock to preserve enough charge for manual entry.

**Replacing.** Slide the interior cover upward to release it. Replace all four
cells together. The lock keeps its access codes and pairing during a battery
change; there is no need to re-pair.

**Dead battery.** Hold a 9V battery against the two contacts under the exterior
keypad to power it long enough to enter your code.
