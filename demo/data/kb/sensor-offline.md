# Sensor offline

Window, door, motion and water sensors report in every 30 minutes. A sensor is
marked offline after it misses three consecutive check-ins.

**Common causes.** A depleted coin cell (CR2032, about two years), the sensor
being knocked out of range, or the hub having moved.

**What to try.**

1. Replace the coin cell. Twist the cover counter-clockwise to open.
2. Press the pairing button once; the LED blinks blue while it rejoins.
3. Power-cycle the hub: unplug for 15 seconds and allow two minutes to settle.

If the sensor stays offline after a power-cycle and a battery change, we open a
ticket and a technician runs field diagnostics. There is no charge for this on
an active plan.
