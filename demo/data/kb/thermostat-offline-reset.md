# Resetting a HomeSense thermostat

If a thermostat is unresponsive, showing a blank screen, or reporting offline in
the app, a reset clears most faults.

**Soft reset (try this first).** Press and hold the dial for 10 seconds until
the ring turns amber, then release. The thermostat reboots and rejoins Wi-Fi in
about 60 seconds. Your schedule and target temperature are kept.

**Factory reset.** Hold the dial for 30 seconds until the ring turns red. This
erases the schedule and Wi-Fi credentials, and the thermostat must be re-added
in the app. Only do this if a soft reset did not help.

If the thermostat still shows offline after a soft reset, check that the hub is
powered and within 40 feet. If it remains offline after both steps, we open a
support ticket for field diagnostics.
